Freeze a finite `work-on` Validation-surface manifest before implementation is delegated. The closability gate now accepts a criterion's direct-evidence population only as an explicit finite enumeration or a deterministic, non-interpretive selection rule that it evaluates during preflight, freezes the resulting manifest as recoverable run-local state identified with the trusted snapshot, selected workflow, and pre-implementation base, and recomputes the whole preflight rather than patching one entry when an input changes. The selected workflow carries the manifest to the implementation delegate and every review context, keeps it bounding evidence rather than implementation, review, or same-mechanism investigation scope, and fails closed when a trusted criterion turns out to require an omitted member: `Closes` becomes unavailable, the omission is recorded and classified instead of remediated in-run, and the run hands back as `Progresses` or `failed`.
