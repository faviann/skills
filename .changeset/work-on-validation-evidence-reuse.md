Let fresh `work-on` reviewers reuse qualifying raw validation evidence for an
exact unchanged candidate while making their own Independent judgments. The
workflow now binds evidence to complete Candidate, Validation, environment,
toolchain, external-input, and artifact identity; preserves safe recoverable
provenance and inspectable results; requires the narrowest Independent execution
for a concrete unresolved assurance question; carries qualifying evidence
across reviewer stages and final closeout; keeps failures blocking until
adjudicated; and treats unsafe or unrecoverable evidence as non-reusable without
adding telemetry or another evidence subsystem. Assurance need alone decides
when a validation must run again; a separate efficiency directive, reachable
only after sufficiency is settled, stops materially costly repetition without
classifying validation cost at runtime.
